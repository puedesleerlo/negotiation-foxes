## `banks2020adve` — Adversarial risk analysis: An overview (2020)

- DOI: 10.1002/wics.1530
- PDF: paywalled · **no open-access route**: the notes come from the Kosmos report, not from the original

What the Kosmos report attributes to it:

> Adversarial Risk Analysis (Banks et al., 2022) explicitly separates aleatory uncertainty (randomness in outcomes conditional on both parties' choices) from epistemic uncertainty (incomplete knowledge of the opponent's utility function, beliefs, and resources).

> ARA models the latter through subjective probability distributions over the opponent's attributes and uses Monte Carlo simulation to propagate these uncertainties ([9.1], [9.2]).
