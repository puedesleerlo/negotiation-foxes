# Public information about the case

What both parties know, and know the other knows. Anything not here and not in your own
confidential corpus, you do not know.

## The press

- Harbor Print is closing its offset line and is selling a used two-color offset press.
- The press is twelve years old, was serviced last spring and runs without faults.
- Linden Books, a small publisher across town, currently outsources its print runs and has
  asked about buying the press.

## Market value

- Used presses of this model change hands rarely, and condition varies a great deal, so the few
  listings that exist are a weak guide to price.

## Rules of the negotiation

- The deal is **for cash**, and the buyer collects the press. The only term negotiated is the
  price.
